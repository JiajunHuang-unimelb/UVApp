- Group number, name, student number, and email of every student
- One-page itemised breakdown of potential contributions by each student
- Describe how to meet every rubric criterion for assignment, with justification
- Check rubric in LMS

This assignment serves as a checkpoint to ensure every group is on track to complete the project. For this checkpoint, every group needs to upload a report (PDF) that contains:

Your group number, as well as the name, student number, and email of every student.
- A one-page itemised breakdown of the potential contributions by each student.
- A section of up to 10 pages that describes how you plan to meet every rubric criterion for the Assignment. In this section you need to provide justification as to how any why your plan fulfils every criterion in the rubric.

## Milestone 2 (70%)

Week 11 - Main deliverable (60%): Software, Report, Video, Github repo
Week 12 - In-person viva (10%): During tutorials, one student at a time, questions about code committed in Github

Viva rubric:
- Scope of Contribution: how much contribution (4%)
- Authorship: demonstrate code is yours, decisions made (3%)
- Understanding: can explain code and how to change (3%)

Every group needs to build a mobile app that MUST:
- Run on phone
- Use multiple sensors
- Access information from Internet/cloud
- Be usable
- Be innovative
- NOT be trivial to build (not a weekend project)

 Examples:
 - Game (Flappy Bird with Sensory input and a game server)
 - Utility app (Smart City app: use publicly available APIs (e.g. public transport) and your GPS location, and do something interesting
 - Social app (Foursquare/friends nearby, sharing your context (e.g. my phone is set to silent), location, nearby Points-of-Interest).

The University may choose to promote/advertise exceptional work submitted by students, and therefore parts of your submission may end up on its website or other platforms it uses for the sole purpose of promotion. If you wish to allow your work to receive this kind of publicity, please include this message in your report's cover page: "We authorise the University of Melbourne to use material from our submission for publicity."  Your decision does not affect the grading of your submission in any way.

Please ensure the following items are included in your submission as a single **ZIP file labelled as:  [COMP90018 - Assignment - Group #.zip].**:
1. A report (PDF) that contains:
    - Your group number, as well as the name, student number, email, and Github handle of every student.
    - A link to a YouTube video (up to 10 minutes) demonstrating your App functioning, showcasing each feature in the marking rubric.
    - A set of instructions that can help us compile and run your project.

    - A one-page itemised breakdown of the contributions by each student. **We calculate a score for each student individually**.

Viva is a hurdle (must pass to pass subject)
Project code receive a basis score shared by group: $a$ (up to 60%)
